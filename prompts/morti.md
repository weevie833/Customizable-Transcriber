**Entry 19**
Latin Transcription:
Anno Domini Millesimo Septingentesimo quadraginta primo, die videlicet Decimanona Augusti. Fulvia Pellicorio quondam Luca Morcavallo vidua hujus Parochiæ ætatis suæ septuaginta circiter in communione Sanctæ Matris Ecclesiæ animam suam de repente Deo reddidit nulla sacramenta recepit; cujus corpus eodem præfato die in hac Parochiali Ecclesia Sanctæ Mariæ Majoris humatum fuit. et ad fidem — Januarius Siragolo Parochus

English Translation:
In the year of the Lord one thousand seven hundred and forty one, on the day namely the nineteenth of August. Fulvia Pellicorio, wife of the late Luca Marcavallo, widow, of this parish, of her age seventy approximately, in the communion of Holy Mother Church, her soul suddenly to God returned; no sacraments she received; whose body on the same aforementioned day in this parochial church of Saint Mary the Greater was buried. And in witness of the truth — Gennaro Siragolo, Pastor]]
