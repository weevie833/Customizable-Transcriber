Notes: Regression fixture for the parseGodparents "wife of"/"husband of" bug (see CLAUDE.md "Known bug"). Latin Transcription:

Vincentius filius Stephani et Antoniae de Chiara.

English Translation:

On the 26th day of September 1752. I baptized an infant born today to the spouses Stephanus and Antonia de Chiara, and was given the name Vincentius. The godfather was Cajetanus Ragusa; the godmother was Rosa Creddo, wife of Gregorius.
