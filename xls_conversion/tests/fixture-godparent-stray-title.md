Alexander Maria Joseph filius Pauli et Annae Mariae Ragusa. Die 2: 7bris 1753. Ego Sac.s D. Andreas Giarratana Cap.us ord.us hujus Majo: Paroch: Eccl.ae sub titulo S.ti Nicolai Clusae bap.vi Infan.em heri mane natum ex Paulo et Anna Maria Ragusa Jug. cui nomen imposui Alexander Maria Joseph P.P. fuerunt D.r D. English Translation:

On the 2nd day of September 1753. I, Don Andreas Giarratana, priest, ordinary Chaplain of this Mother Parish Church under the title of Saint Nicolaus at Chiusa, baptized an infant born yesterday morning to the spouses Paulus and Anna Maria Ragusa, and was given the name Alexander Maria Joseph. The godfather was Doctor Don Carolus Calsi; the godmother was Don Rosalia di Bello.
