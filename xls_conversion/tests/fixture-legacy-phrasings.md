Entry 1: Manfre, Filippa Maria

Date: Die 23. 7bris

Latin Transcription:

Filippa Maria filia M.ri Joseph et Annae M.ae Manfre.

English Translation:

On the 23rd day of September. I, Don Vitus Musso, baptized an infant born today to Josephus and Anna Maria Manfre, married couple, upon whom was imposed the name Filippa Maria. The godparents were Josephus Guagliano and Rosa Zito.
